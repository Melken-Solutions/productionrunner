# Produce and ship in stages

Start in New production request, beside the total quantity. Choose **Ship all together** or **Produce & ship in stages**. Staged deliveries have one quantity and required ship date per row, with **Add another shipment** and a live scheduled/remaining total. Put shipments in date order. Shipments cannot exceed the order quantity or fall after its required date.

A draft may leave quantity unscheduled. Approval requires the entire quantity to be scheduled. Schedule-only edits invalidate an existing approval, just like other request changes. Existing requests can add the schedule through Edit request details before conversion. Older integrations omitting schedule fields preserve the existing schedule on edit.

The approved schedule copies into the JobRunner. Staged deliveries require production lots or independent serial units; whole-batch conversion is rejected. Production lot size remains separate from shipment size: two 50-piece lots can supply one 100-piece shipment. The schedule does not create an allocation of specific lots to shipments or waive route, inspection, label, packing or ERP requirements.

For 580 ordered with shipments of 50, 100 and 430, the first target is 50. After 50 actually depart, the next target automatically becomes 100. After another 50 depart, that target shows 50 remaining. Inspection or packing alone never advances a shipment target. Departure reversals recalculate progress and can reopen an earlier target. Customer returns retain their separate workflow.

Order progress and packing pages display the schedule. The next outstanding target appears on travelers, station work, shipping planning, and work and packing queues, with HOT priority when present. No automatic notifications or carrier labels are generated by a schedule.

After conversion, planners/admins can edit the **remaining delivery schedule** on the order or packing page. The form deducts already-departed quantities and requires the revised schedule to cover all units still owed. Old schedules and departures remain in history. Stale form versions, unauthorized users and cross-organization access are rejected. The older single-target endpoint remains available for jobs without a full schedule, but cannot silently replace a published schedule.

Schedules use tenant-scoped, audited delivery_schedule_saved events in the existing request and ticket event stores. Request approval and ticket version locks guard edits. No database migration is required. Existing jobs and production history are not automatically rewritten.
