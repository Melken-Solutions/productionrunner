# Repeatable planning

Target: prepare a repeat job in under two minutes, with approval and release controls intact. This is a usability target, not a measured guarantee. New/custom parts need an approved plan first.

Admins maintain **Operation library** instructions, acceptance checks, first-piece requirements, DXF requirements and applicable stations. Multiple stations can share one operation (for example Laser 1 and Laser 2). Unrestricted templates appear at every station; the suggested station must belong to the selected stations. Changes create a version and do not rewrite saved requests or released jobs.

Starter operations include laser cutting, drilling, countersinking, counterboring, tapping, deburring, bending, marking, independent inspection, packing and shipping. Starter station suggestions use matching station names; admins should review and save their shop's associations. These are planning choices, not equipment qualifications. Drawing dimensions, tolerances and tooling must be supplied or referenced explicitly, never inferred by software. Outside processing still uses the controlled vendor plan.

For a new plan, choose the station, then a standard operation. Instructions and checks copy into the step. Choose another operation at the same station by adding another step. Changing station does not erase existing instructions; incompatible or older saved selections show a review message.

For repeat work, choose the PN/revision. Its approved part plan loads the material, ordered route, instructions, checks and outside requirements. Enter quantity, delivery date, customer/destination and applicable ERP references. **Review repeat job** goes directly to the review; the Job Plan tab remains available for exceptions. Review warnings and save. Required document approval, ERP clearance and release gates remain in place. No order-specific dates, quantities or references are copied from a previous job.
