# Pieces produced and outside purchase orders

Admin → Pieces produced provides a UTC date range (up to 366 days), part selection, and CSV export. The default measure is final-inspection accepted pieces, including first successful rework acceptance. Whole batches use their piece quantity, serial travelers count one, and lots use their quantities. Operation completions can be filtered by step: these count pieces processed at each step, not unique finished pieces. A 50-piece lot completing two steps represents 100 piece-operation completions but only 50 accepted pieces after final inspection.

Lot splits redistribute inherited completion evidence without increasing the total. Failed inspections do not count as acceptance. Later scrap does not remove earlier recorded throughput. This report does not estimate raw sheet consumption, unrecorded overproduction, net inventory, or repeated repair cycles. Deleted jobs no longer contribute; cancelled jobs retain their recorded work. CSV fields are escaped against spreadsheet formulas. Reports are organization-scoped and admin-only.

Outside processing exposes Purchase Order Number on the request and job plan using the existing reference storage, preserving prior values. It is optional during planning and shipment, since not every organization uses vendor POs. Each Send parts to vendor form defaults to the plan PO and permits a shipment-specific number. The send audit records an immutable PO snapshot, displayed beside that shipment. Previous shipments without a snapshot show Not recorded rather than guessing from a current plan. Reusable part templates and repeat jobs continue to clear order-specific references. ProductionRunner does not generate or submit ERP purchase orders.
