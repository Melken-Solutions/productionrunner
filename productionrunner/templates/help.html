PRODUCTIONRUNNER / HELP

From request to delivery

Use the job’s released plan and current drawing revision at every station. Your assigned roles determine which actions you can perform.

Start with Today

Planners and admins use Today for planned work, due shipments, HOT jobs, holds, readiness issues and open help requests. Calendar assigns work days to jobs or individual travelers; work days do not change ship dates or authorize production. Whole-job dates apply unless a traveler has its own override.

Quantity progress counts each active unit once. Completed-through-step quantities are cumulative. Use Release readiness on an unreleased Job Plan, and Make this part again on a catalog part to reuse its approved plan.

In a help conversation, choose I’m handling this to claim ownership. Connection and save status show whether the page is connected and whether an action has been confirmed. A connected page is not automatically a refreshed overview; use Refresh overview for current Today data.

ERP order references & clearance

Organization Admin → ERP & demand settings defines request paths, reference labels, required stages and clear-to-build checks. Standard production orders can omit a sales order; supporting-sheet demand can require the ERP SO, line and sheet reference. Drafts may be incomplete. Record manual ERP clearance from the job before release. ProductionRunner does not query SAP automatically.

Customer PO, ERP sales order, ERP production order and JobRunner ID remain separate. References carry onto travelers and timing exports. Released jobs retain their reviewed settings. HOT priority and shipping choices remain separate. Operation and reason mappings are review notes; station scrap/overproduction accounting is not added by this configuration.

1. Plan the request

  1. Open Requests → New production request. Choose the PN/revision and total quantity. Select the material and enter the order’s required date.
  2. Beside quantity, choose Ship all together or Produce & ship in stages. For staged delivery, add each shipment quantity and ship date in date order. The balance shows how many units remain to schedule.
  3. Build the ordered Job Plan using configured stations and operation instructions. Review any saved part template; include outside work, inbound inspection, final independent inspection, packing and shipping when applicable.
  4. Upload missing drawing/DXF files for the correct revision. Choose Save draft for unfinished work, or Save & continue to release when ready. An incomplete staged schedule cannot be approved; its quantities must equal the total order quantity.
  5. On Release to floor, review the drawing package, Job Plan and ERP checks, choose quantity tracking, and optionally select Save this plan as a reusable template. Confirm once and choose Approve & release to floor. The system records document approval, template publication, JobRunner creation, ERP clearance and release separately. Missing requirements block release; a failure leaves saved progress available for correction.

Editing the request’s schedule invalidates its approval. Ship dates cannot be later than the order’s required date. Incomplete drafts can be saved for later.

Open requests

2. Produce and ship in stages

Example: 580 ordered → 50, 100 and 430 scheduled for separate dates. After 50 physically depart, the 100-piece shipment becomes the target automatically. If another 50 depart, that target shows 50 still to ship. The order stays open until all 580 have left.

A production lot is not a shipment. A 100-piece shipment can contain two independently tracked 50-piece lots. Keep each lot traveler with its material. Before completing fewer pieces than a traveler contains, stop the work session and split the lot through its partial-run controls. Some inspected, packed, vendor-tracked or reworked lots require controlled disposition instead of a split.

HOT is priority, not shipping clearance. Accepted → Packed → Shipped shows progress against the next outstanding target. Production and inspection of the remaining order can continue while accepted lots ship.

After creation, planners/admins can use Produce & ship in stages → Edit remaining delivery schedule on the order or packing page. Schedule all units still owed and choose a reason. Original schedules and departures remain in history. Reversing an incorrect departure can reopen an earlier target.

Older whole-batch jobs must be unstarted to convert to lots. The system does not invent prior production or shipping records, automatically split lots to match a schedule, or assign specific lots to shipments.

3. Work at each station

Need remote help? Choose Ask planner / admin on the station traveler. Choose a predefined message, review it and send without typing; the job and traveler are attached. New messages show a visible banner with Open messages and Dismiss while the app is open. Dismiss does not mark messages read; the unread badge remains until you open the conversation. Alerts refresh within about 15 seconds. Choose Other / type a note for a custom message. Messaging does not pause or hold production.

On a tablet, use Shop Floor. Choose your station and open a ready traveler matching your material. This browser remembers the station. Working, ready, upcoming and held work are separated; the station TV shows the same readiness. Idle queues refresh every 15 seconds without interrupting an open work form. After completion, follow the next-station handoff. Operators without planner/admin roles land here after sign-in; combined-role users can open Shop Floor from the main navigation. Released internal work opens directly in station controls.

  1. Open or scan the lot/unit traveler. Unassigned released internal work can be picked up at an active station; choose the station before Start. Verify the current material, drawing, DXF and work instructions.
  2. Start work. Pause for an interruption, Resume to continue, or Stop to release the station without completing the operation. Another authorized operator can cover the work; the actual person is recorded.
  3. Choose Finish & confirm quantity when ready. Select the completed quantity and verification result. Routine checks need no typing; required measurements or check-sheet references still need evidence. Finish only after confirming the traveler’s completed quantity and required evidence. Follow the route in order and complete required first-piece checks.
  4. Keep nonconforming material segregated and use the hold/rework controls. Final acceptance requires an independent inspector.

Inspection results and measurements still require actual evidence. A predefined change reason does not substitute for a production or inspection check.

4. Pack, obtain labels and record departure

  1. Open Packing & dispatch and select accepted lots. Complete applicable ERP checkpoints for those lots and record physical packaging.
  2. Use Shipping plan, boxes & labels for destination, box identification and shipment/pallet preparation. A pallet may contain different jobs going to the same destination.
  3. Request labels through the shipping handoff. The planner prepares the labels in the applicable external system and uploads the PDF packet. The packer downloads and prints the current packet.
  4. Complete the shipping checks and record departure only when the goods physically leave the building.

Setting a shipment date does not create carrier labels, perform SAP transactions or send email/push alerts. Downloading a label does not prove it was printed. Customer returns use the separate return workflow; do not reverse a valid original shipment to record a return.

Administration and account help

Admins can use Switch view in the header to jump between Admin, Planning, Jobs, Shop Floor, TV boards, Packing, Shipping, Parts, Messages and Reports. Admin-only accounts can inspect station travelers in Admin overview; production controls still require the Operator role.

Admins and planners can review permanent job deletion from the job ticket’s Delete job section. Confirmation requires the JobRunner ID and a reason. Shared files remain; the original request is cancelled and a deletion audit is retained. Stop active work first; shipping-load and return/repair links can prevent deletion.

Organization Admin → Message snippets lets admins add, edit and disable custom request and reply messages. Previously sent messages keep their original wording. Organization admins configure people and roles, branding, stations, material choices, vendors, operation templates, workflow requirements and shipping settings. Getting started provides the setup checklist. Users may have multiple roles; admin access alone does not grant every production permission.

Choose a predefined reason when changing controlled information. Additional details are optional; Other requires an explanation. Use Account security to manage your own credentials. Create shared TV logins using People & access → Add TV screen; no person or email is required. Shared TVs use a dedicated display-only account, which stays signed in through daily use with automatic renewal; a planner/admin session is for interactive work.

For operators, People & access → Manage → Primary station sets the starting station for the operator’s Today view on sign-in. Today shows current work, planned work, HOT and ready jobs. On Today or Shop Floor, Change station offers large buttons for moving around the shop. Switching queues does not move or pause active work, or change permissions.

People & access → Manage offers Revoke access for temporary removal and Delete account from this organization for permanent removal. Deletion requires the exact username and a reason. Deleted accounts disappear from the directory and cannot be restored; historical attribution and the reserved username remain. Other organizations are unaffected.

Bulk drawing import supports selecting a folder with all subfolders. CSV references can use relative paths or unique filenames. Files not listed in the CSV are excluded in the preview instead of blocking the batch. Review the excluded files and confirm the matches before importing.

For repeat work, choose a PN with an approved saved plan, enter this order’s details, and select Review repeat job. For new plans, choose each station and then a standard operation to populate instructions and checks. Admins manage station-specific choices in Operation library. Required approvals still apply.

To repeat an actual previous run, open its ticket or lot dashboard and select Run this job again. Enter the new quantity, date and ERP references, then create and review the draft. Its original-job link preserves traceability. Prior approvals, production results and shipping records stay with the original job.

Before reporting a problem, note the JobRunner/request ID, affected lot, screen and what you expected. Never include passwords or PINs.
