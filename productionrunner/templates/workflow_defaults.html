Request and document readiness

Recommended for a consistent request-to-departure process. Custom requests always require PDF and DXF review; standard requests require their catalog drawing. This setting cannot waive those approval rules. If unchecked, direct ticket creation remains available and does not imply document approval.

Up to 20 distinct checkpoints, 120 characters each. Leave both lists empty for no ERP gates. Physical packing, departure confirmation and independent production inspection still apply. Checkpoints cover the entire ticket and can only be completed after the entire ticket passes inspection; they can therefore delay partial serial shipments.

Both examples require approved requests. The ERP example adds production confirmation before packing and a delivery/transfer reference before departure. Adapt the names to your shop's actual process.
